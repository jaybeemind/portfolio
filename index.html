05 / Independent project

ROOC Guild Management

A working application for guild rosters, loot auctions, and rewards. I designed and built the system from the workflow through to deployment.

The design decision
Give each auction a fixed lifecycle and preserve its history. Finalized assignments stop being editable, and role-based permissions control who can make changes.
What it supports
Member rosters, reward catalogs, auction results, and an append-only audit log.
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Role-based access
  • Audit history
